1. Introduction

This Cookie Notice sets out how Coin Bridge AG (“We”, “Our”, “Us”, “Kai”) uses cookies on our users’ (“You”, “User”) website(s) and mobile application (collectively, the “Platform”).

Coin Bridge AG a subsidiary of D.L. Certify Global Group Ltd. (Cyprus) is a Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) incorporated in the Canton of Zug, Switzerland, and regulated under membership of VQF - Verein zur Qualitätssicherung von Finanzdienstleistungen, a self-regulatory organisation (SRO) recognised by the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA). Coin Bridge AG processes personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP; SR 235.1) and applicable FDPIC guidance.

2. About Cookies

Cookies are small, text-based files that are stored locally on your browser or device to enable a wide range of functions. They help us to remember details about your visit, making it easier for you to return and enjoy a more personalised and useful experience.

When you accept cookies on our Platform, we download this small file onto your device, which assists us in providing the best possible service to you. However, not all cookies are optional — some are strictly necessary for access to and operation of the Platform.

3. How Do We Use Cookies?

Below is an explanation of the types and functions of cookies we use:

Functional Cookies

These cookies make our Platform work properly and remember your preferences. They:

  • Store choices such as language or display settings;
  • Keep session information, including pending trades;
  • Help run the features you request; and
  • Improve product performance and reliability.

Security Cookies

These necessary cookies are used to keep your account and data safe. They:

  • Authenticate users and block unauthorised access;
  • Help prevent spam, fraud, and abuse; and
  • Track service outages and help identify potential causes.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how you use our services and Platform. They:

  • Measure site traffic and user engagement; and
  • Improve service design and content quality.

Marketing and Advertising Cookies

These cookies display and help measure advertisements and marketing performance on our Platform. They:

  • Display personalised or general advertisements;
  • Limit how often you see the same advertisements; and
  • Measure advertisement performance and conversion rates.

Personalisation Cookies

These cookies are used to tailor your experience and recommendations. They:

  • Show relevant content and search results;
  • Customise suggestions and recommendations; and
  • May use your location (if enabled by you).

Even if you disable personalisation cookies, some features may still adapt based on your language, device type, or location.

5. Cookie Management

Ultimately, the choice of whether to accept optional cookies is yours. However, declining optional cookies may materially affect the quality of the service available to you on our Platform. Below are steps you may take to manage cookies on your devices:

Browser Settings

On most browsers, you can control how cookies are stored and used during your browsing session. You can also delete cookies and clear your browsing data at any time. For instance, in Google Chrome, you can manage these settings at chrome://settings/cookies to:

  • Delete existing cookies;
  • Allow or block cookies entirely; and
  • Set cookie preferences for specific websites.

Chrome also offers Incognito mode, which automatically clears cookies and browsing history from those private windows once you close them.

Other Applications

You can control how device identifiers and similar technologies are used to recognise your device or application activity. For instance, you can manage the Advertising ID on Android or Apple’s Advertising Identifier (IDFA) in your device’s privacy settings. Application-specific identifiers can usually be managed directly within the application’s Privacy or Security settings, allowing you to limit tracking or reset identifiers whenever you choose.

Third-Party Cookies

  • Coin Bridge AG may use cookies and similar technologies set by trusted third-party partners to enhance your experience and improve our services on the Platform. These cookies are not placed directly by Coin Bridge AG but by external providers that help deliver certain features or integrations.
  • Third-party cookies are used for purposes including the following:
    • To understand how users interact with our Platform and improve performance (e.g. Google Analytics);
    • To deliver relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness (e.g. Google Ads, Meta Ads); and
    • To enable embedded content or integrations such as chat support, payment gateways, or social media features (e.g. support videos hosted on YouTube).
  • You can manage or disable optional third-party cookies through your device or application settings. Please note that blocking these cookies may affect certain Platform features, such as personalised content, analytics insights, or advertisement relevance.
  • Third-party providers are responsible for how they use the information they collect through their cookies. We encourage you to review their respective privacy or cookie policies for more details on how your data is processed by them.

6. Legal Basis for Cookie Processing

In accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP; SR 235.1) and applicable FDPIC guidance, Coin Bridge AG processes personal data collected through cookies on the following bases:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: processed on the basis of Coin Bridge AG’s legitimate interests in operating a secure and functional Platform, and to fulfil its contractual obligations to users;
  • Analytics, personalisation, and marketing cookies: processed on the basis of your freely given, specific, and informed consent, which you may withdraw at any time through your cookie preferences or browser settings.

Where Coin Bridge AG relies on consent for cookie processing, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

Coin Bridge AG is a member of VQF - Verein zur Qualitätssicherung von Finanzdienstleistungen (https://www.vqf.ch), a self-regulatory organisation recognised by the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA) under the Swiss Anti-Money Laundering Act. Coin Bridge AG is subject to VQF supervision in respect of its anti-money-laundering obligations. This affiliation does not constitute a banking licence or FINMA approval of the products offered. Coin Bridge AG is not a bank and is not directly supervised by FINMA.

KAI is a financial technology platform. Banking and fiat account services, where available, are provided by third-party banking or financial institution partners under their applicable terms. Coin Bridge AG does not itself accept or hold public deposits. The relevant service provider and contractual arrangements are identified in the documentation applicable to each service.

Balances held with Coin Bridge AG are not covered by the Swiss deposit protection scheme. Any protection applicable to eligible deposits held with a partner bank depends on the relevant bank, jurisdiction, account structure and applicable conditions. Such protection does not automatically extend to all balances accessible through KAI.

Digital assets are not covered by Swiss deposit protection and may lose some or all of their value.
